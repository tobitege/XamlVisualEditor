## Microsoft Fluent System Icons

The layout toolbar uses SVG path data from `@fluentui/svg-icons` version `1.1.341`, regular 20px variants.

Source: https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-system-icons

Included icons: `align_left`, `align_center_vertical`, `align_right`, `align_top`, `align_center_horizontal`, `align_bottom`, `align_space_evenly_horizontal`, `align_space_evenly_vertical`, `arrow_autofit_width`, `arrow_autofit_height`, and `grid`.

The center icon names describe the reference line, not the direction of movement.
The three disjoint paths of `align_space_evenly_vertical` are combined without changing their coordinates.
